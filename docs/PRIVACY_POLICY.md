_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

BeamRunner ("the Game") is developed and published by Kick Stands Up LLC ("we", "us").
This policy explains what information is processed when you play the Game on Android or iOS, who processes it, and
the choices you have. If you have a question, contact us at chris.dornan4@gmail.com.

**In short:** we do not run servers, we do not have player accounts, and we never receive your name, e-mail address
or payment details. Your progress stays on your device. The Game shows ads, and the ad providers described below
collect some device information to do so. Some optional features (sign-in, cloud save, crash reports) involve
Google, Apple or our crash-reporting provider, and each one is described below.

## Information stored on your device

Your progress (scores, statistics, unlocked ships, missions, settings) is stored **on your device**. We do not
operate servers that receive it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it (unless you use cloud save, see below). A few
device-only preferences (your reminder and crash-report choices, when the Game last asked for a rating) are stored the
same way. The save file carries a signature so that edited files are not loaded; it contains no personal data.

## Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads)

The Game shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay, Unity Technologies' ad mediation platform ("LevelPlay"), which
requests ads from Unity Ads and any other ad networks enabled for the Game: optional rewarded videos (for example to
continue a run) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. LevelPlay and the ad networks may collect and process:

* device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID, or the iOS IDFA only if you allow tracking), device and
  OS information;
* approximate location derived from your IP address;
* information about ads you view and interact with.

This is used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. On first launch the Game asks whether you allow
**personalised** ads; on iOS you are then asked by the system whether the Game may track you (App Tracking
Transparency). You can change the in-game choice at any time in **Settings › Privacy**, and the iOS choice in the
device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you decline either, ads are not personalised. Unity processes
this data under its own privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Purchasing **Remove Ads** disables full-screen ads between runs. Rewarded videos remain available because you
choose to watch them.

## In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The Game receives a confirmation of the purchase
(product and transaction identifiers) on your device so that it can deliver what you bought; we do not receive your
payment details.

## Google Play Games and Game Center (when available and you sign in)

If you are signed in to **Google Play Games** (Android) or **Game Center** (iOS), the Game submits your scores to
the Game's leaderboards and unlocks its achievements through that service, and shows the service's own leaderboard
and achievement screens. Google or Apple process your gamer profile (name/avatar, player ID) and these results under
their own privacy policies (https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and show them
to other players according to your settings in their apps. The Game only receives your display name (shown in
**Settings**) and uses a one-way hash of your player ID on your device to tell accounts apart; neither is sent to us.
Signing in is optional: on Android you can decline or sign in later in **Settings**; on iOS you manage Game Center in
the device's Settings app.

## Cloud save (when available)

Cloud save keeps a copy of your progress (the same data as the save file above, plus a random identifier for each
of your devices so that gems are counted correctly across devices) in **your own** storage: Google Play Games
Saved Games on Android, or iCloud (key-value storage of your Apple Account) on iOS. We cannot read it. It syncs when
the Game starts, when it goes to the background and when you tap **Settings › Sync now**, and it is combined with the
progress on your device so nothing is lost. To delete it, remove the Game's data in the Google Play Games app
(Settings › Delete Play Games account and data / the Game's saved data) or in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud ›
Manage Storage.

## Crash reports (Sentry) — only when available and you allow them

If your version of the Game offers **Send crash reports** (on the first-launch privacy screen or in
**Settings › Privacy**) and you switch it on, the Game sends a report to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry
(Functional Software, Inc.), when it crashes, freezes or hits an internal error. A report contains: what went wrong
(error message, the Game's call stack and a short log of recent in-game events), the Game's version, the device
model, OS version, memory/CPU/graphics details, the time and the country-level locale and time zone, and a random
installation identifier created by the crash reporter (used to count how many players are affected; it is **not**
an advertising identifier and is not linked to your identity). Reports contain **no** screenshots, no names, e-mail
or account details, no advertising IDs and no precise location; we have configured Sentry not to store IP
addresses. Reports are used only to find and fix bugs and are deleted automatically after no more than 90 days.

Crash reports are **off until you choose**. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and whenever your region cannot be
told from your device's settings) the switch starts off and nothing is sent unless you turn it on (your consent,
GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) / ePrivacy Art. 5(3)). Elsewhere it starts on, and you can turn it off before playing or at any
time (our legitimate interest in keeping the Game stable). Turning it off stops reporting immediately. Sentry
processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement, with standard contractual clauses for transfers
outside the EEA: https://sentry.io/privacy/.

## Reminders (local notifications)

If you accept **Daily reminders** (offered once in the Game, after you claim your first daily reward, or in
**Settings › Reminders**), the Game asks your device for permission to show notifications and schedules at most
one reminder at a time — around 19:00 your local time, never at night — when your next daily reward (or a streak)
is waiting. Reminders are created and shown **by your device**; no data is sent anywhere and no push service is
used. Turn them off in **Settings › Reminders** or in your device's notification settings.

## Ratings

From time to time (never on your first day, at most once a month) the Game may ask Google Play or the App Store to
show their standard rating prompt. Whether it appears, and what you rate, is handled by the store; the Game does
not learn your rating.

## What we do not do

* We do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect it for our own marketing.
* We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own; Unity's built-in analytics and engine diagnostics are
  switched off in the Game.
* The Game does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Under some US state privacy laws, showing personalised ads can count as "sharing" personal information for
targeted advertising. You can opt out at any time by turning off personalised ads in **Settings › Privacy** (and,
on iOS, by not allowing tracking); you can also limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your device's
settings. The Game does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because it is not a website; use the in-game
setting instead.

## Data retention and security

Data on your device stays there until you reset your progress or uninstall the Game. Cloud-save copies stay in your
Google or Apple account until you delete them. Crash reports are deleted after no more than 90 days. Data collected by
Unity, Google, Apple or ad networks is retained under their own policies. Information sent from the Game to these
services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through
the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Your choices and rights

* Change the personalised-ads setting and **Send crash reports** in **Settings › Privacy**.
* Turn **Daily reminders** on or off in **Settings › Reminders** (or in your device's notification settings).
* Reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
* Delete all local data with **Settings › Reset progress** or by uninstalling the Game. With cloud save, a reset is
  also applied to the cloud copy at the next sync.
* Sign in or out of Google Play Games / Game Center, and delete their data, in their apps or your device settings.
* For requests regarding data held by Unity / LevelPlay or other ad networks, use their data-privacy tools.
* Crash reports are not linked to you; to have reports from your device deleted, contact us with the approximate
  date and device model.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access,
correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and
to complain to your data-protection authority. Because we do not hold personal information on servers of our own,
most requests are best made directly to the services named above; we will answer any request sent to us and help
where we can.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at the same
address. If a change materially affects how your information is used, we will also point it out in the Game or in
its store listing.
